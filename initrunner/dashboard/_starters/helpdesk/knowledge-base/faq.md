# Frequently asked questions

## How do I reset my password?

1. Open the company portal sign-in page.
2. Choose **Forgot password**.
3. Enter your work email. You will receive a reset link that expires in
   30 minutes.
4. Set a new password (at least 12 characters).

If you do not receive the email, check spam or ask IT to confirm your
account email.

## How do I file a ticket?

Email `helpdesk@example.com` or use the internal portal. Include:

- Subject: a one-line summary
- What you expected vs what happened
- Time of the issue and any error messages

Urgent outages: call the on-call number listed on the IT intranet home
page.
